# BO 500-Trial Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Track steps below.

**Goal:** Expand the complete BO lifecycle from 200 to 500 total trials.
**Architecture:** Shared capacity constants across schemas/calculations/readers;
transactional SQLite constraint migration; aligned UI guards and count errors.
**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, SQLite, NumPy/sklearn, React/TypeScript, Playwright.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-bo-500-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Maximum 500 total trials; no silent sampling, data coercion or partial batches.
- Preserve old artifact bytes, equations, budgets and Python 3.10 compatibility.
- One heavy test/build at a time; no production dependency changes.
- Do not modify the running user preview or its workspace during validation.

## Review Focus

- A 240-row import must not fail later in snapshot/read/report validation.
- Repeat/conflict counts may exceed the old derived 199/100 limits.
- A budget of 500 must not silently make an old saved budget of 200 become 500.
- 499 plus batch size two must roll back, and closed studies remain closed.
- SQLite table rebuilds must preserve foreign keys, indexes and artifact owners.
