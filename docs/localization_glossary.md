`p-value`, `R`, `VIF`, `PRESS`, and `Cp` retain their notation. `Factor` is
translated by statistical context: an experimental factor is a factor, while a
categorical predictor is described as categorical. Evidence wording must use
"reject" or "fail to reject", never "prove".
